ECB Pattern Leakage Visualizer

Electronic Codebook (ECB) mode encrypts identical plaintext blocks into identical ciphertext blocks. This leaks structural patterns (like the famous Tux Linux penguin image). Let's visualize this with text blocks.

Modern Cipher Playground

Test ChaCha20-Poly1305 (fast, modern stream cipher), AES-GCM (authenticated encryption), and 3DES (legacy).

Authenticated Encryption (GCM / Poly1305):

Encryption ≠ Authentication. An attacker can modify ciphertext in transit (e.g., bit-flipping attacks on CBC). GCM and Poly1305 add a cryptographic MAC (tag) to ensure the ciphertext has not been tampered with.
