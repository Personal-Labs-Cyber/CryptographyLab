How TLS/HTTPS Works

1. The Handshake

When you visit google.com, the server sends its Digital Certificate to your browser.

2. Verification

Your browser checks the certificate's Digital Signature against its built-in list of trusted Root Certificate Authorities (like DigiCert or Let's Encrypt). If the signature is valid, you know the Public Key actually belongs to Google.

3. Key Exchange

Your browser and Google use this verified Public Key to securely agree on a Symmetric Key (using algorithms like Diffie-Hellman / ECDH).

4. Secure Channel

All further communication is encrypted with a fast symmetric cipher (like AES-GCM) using that shared key!
